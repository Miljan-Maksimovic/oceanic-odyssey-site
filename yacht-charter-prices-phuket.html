What sets the price

Set the yachts side by side, and this is what stands between an 89,000 THB day and a 720,000 THB day.

  • Size. The largest yachts carry the highest rates.
  • Guests. Each guest above the rate's number adds a set amount. The rate is for the yacht, so the cost per guest falls as the group grows.
  • Route and season. Both move the rate.
  • On board. A jet ski, a jacuzzi or a butler is in the rate where it is listed.
  • The day. Route by route, the day is on the Places page.

What each rate includes

On every yacht: captain and crew, towels, fruit and snacks, snorkel gear, travel insurance, VAT at 7%. Most also include Thai lunch, water, soft drinks, WiFi and transport for 10 guests, and each yacht's page shows its own list. Beyond that:

  • Tiger 40. An inflatable slider and an island seat, kayaks and a paddle board.
  • Jackie. A paddle board, and fishing on request.
  • Bruna. A paddle board and a slider, an inflatable swimming pool, snorkelling and fishing gear. No Thai lunch is listed.
  • Meraki. A wakeboard, a paddle board, a kneeboard and two towable tubes.
  • Pandora. A paddle board and a kayak.
  • Kefi. A buffet, a swimming pool and a slider, a private jacuzzi, a resident DJ and a photographer.
  • Pearl. A paddle board and a jet ski.
  • Sira. A towable donut, two paddle boards and a floating pool. No Thai lunch is listed.
  • Moonglider. An inflatable swimming pool.
  • Matia Sou. A butler and a private massage therapist, a sea scooter, an inflatable swimming pool, a yacht slide and a jacuzzi, two paddle boards and a jet ski.
  • Simon. A jet ski, two paddle boards and two kayaks, an inflatable slide and a swimming pool.
  • Song of Songs. Thai lunch, food and drink packages, Standard and Premium (Amy sends the menu), a wakeboard, two jet skis, paddleboards and water skis, a jacuzzi, a swimming pool, transport for 12 guests and the national park fees. The Premium package is USD 5,000 more.

A day, a night or two days

  • Overnight. Meraki sleeps 8 guests in 4 cabins, Pandora and Jackie 6 each in 3 cabins, and Simon 10 in five cabins. Overnight rates are quoted per yacht.
  • Two days with a night. Song of Songs, a 42 m superyacht, takes 12 guests in 6 cabins: USD 37,500 for a full day of 8 hours, and USD 46,000 for two full days with one night, both all inclusive.
  • Two or three days on the other yachts. A charter of more than one day is quoted for the yacht and the dates.

Questions about price

How much does it cost to charter a yacht in Thailand?

In Phuket, a private day of 8 hours starts at 89,000 THB on Tiger 40, for up to 10 guests, and at 720,000 THB on Simon, for up to 10, in the Nov to Apr season, the whole yacht for one group. The 42 m Song of Songs is USD 37,500 for the day, all inclusive. This page covers Phuket only.

How much is a 3 day charter on a yacht?

Song of Songs is USD 46,000 for two days and one night, all inclusive. On the other yachts, a longer charter is quoted per yacht.

What does a private yacht in Phuket cost per person?

The rate is for the yacht, so the cost per guest falls as the group grows.

Are rates higher over Christmas and New Year?

Yes. The peak season, 10 Dec to 10 Jan, carries the highest rates on most yachts: Meraki is 280,000 THB for up to 12 guests, against 220,000 THB in Nov to Apr. Kefi and Moonglider are priced the same in every season.

How much does a yacht party cost in Phuket?

A private yacht party is priced like any charter: the whole yacht for 8 hours. Kefi, for up to 100 guests, is 230,000 THB for up to 50, with a resident DJ and photographer in the rate. More on private yacht parties in Phuket.

How long is a full-day charter?

Eight hours, usually 09:00 to 17:00 or 10:00 to 18:00, to suit you. Every day rate on this page is for 8 hours on the water, apart from Moonglider's rates for 4 and 6 hours.
