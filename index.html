Questions

Ten answers, one tap each.

What a day costs

A day of 8 hours is priced for the whole yacht, not per person. From November to April, up to 10 guests start at 89,000 THB on a 40 ft catamaran and at 720,000 THB on the 130 ft Simon.

Read the price guide
What is included

Every yacht page lists captain and crew, towels, fruit and snacks, snorkel gear, travel insurance and VAT at 7%. Most add Thai lunch, water, soft drinks, WiFi and transport for 10 guests. Each page shows its own list.

What each rate includes
Guests and extra guests

Each rate covers a set number of guests: 10 on Matia Sou, 8 on Pearl. Most yachts add a fixed amount for each guest after that, from 1,500 THB on Rose to 10,000 THB on Simon, up to the maximum.

If the weather turns

The sea is calmest from November to April. If the sea is too rough, there is no trip that day. In light rain the captain picks a calmer route, or the day moves to another date, subject to availability.

Message us on WhatsApp or Telegram

Islands in one day

Phi Phi, the 4 Islands, Racha, Hong Island, Maiton and Phang Nga Bay all fit a day of 8 hours. Similan and Surin are overnight charters of 2 to 4 days, planned with Amy case by case.

See every place
When do we leave?

A full day is 8 hours, usually 09:00 to 17:00 or 10:00 to 18:00, to suit you. Amy confirms the meeting point with your date.

National park fees

Park fees are paid by each guest, in cash, at the park. Your captain or hostess helps with the local formalities. Only Song of Songs lists them in its rate.
