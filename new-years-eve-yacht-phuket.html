The evening

On 31 December the sun goes down over Phuket close to 18:20. From then the deck lights come up, the water around the hull goes dark, and the only people on board are your guests and the crew. The guest list and the pace of the night are yours, right up to midnight.

Thai food can be served on board, and Amy takes special requests.

The yacht sets the tone. Meraki has sunbeds on the flybridge and surround sound. Moonglider, the Leopard 90, carries an inflatable swimming pool. Matia Sou carries a butler, a massage therapist and a jacuzzi, all in the rate. Simon, at 130 ft, takes up to 44 guests on one yacht.

Where to spend New Year's Eve in Phuket

Our answer is the water. On a private yacht there are no tickets and no other guests: the deck, the table and the crew belong to your group, and the night runs at your pace. If the evening is also a birthday, see a birthday on a private yacht and private yacht parties in Phuket.

Questions about New Year's Eve on a yacht

How much is a yacht for New Year's Eve in Phuket?

In the peak season, 10 Dec to 10 Jan, a full day of 8 hours on the yachts above runs from 220,000 THB on Jackie, for up to 8 guests, to 820,000 THB on Simon, for up to 10, and the 42 m Song of Songs is USD 37,500 for the day, all inclusive. Every yacht's rate in each season is in the Phuket yacht charter price guide.

How many guests can come?

Up to 10 on Pandora, 15 on Jackie, 25 on Meraki or Matia Sou, and 44 on Simon. For 50 to 100 guests, Kefi, a party yacht built in 2025, is 230,000 THB for up to 50 guests in every season, the peak included, and each guest above 50 adds 4,000 THB.
