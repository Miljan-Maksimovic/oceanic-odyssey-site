Price and booking

What a day costs

How much does a private yacht cost in Phuket?

A day of 8 hours is priced for the whole yacht, not per person. From November to April, up to 10 guests start at 89,000 THB on a 40 ft catamaran and at 720,000 THB on the 130 ft Simon.

Read the price guide

What is included

What is included in the price?

Every charter includes captain and crew, towels, fruit and snacks, snorkel gear, travel insurance and VAT at 7%. Most add Thai lunch, water, soft drinks, WiFi and transport for 10 guests. Your quote lists what your yacht includes.

What each rate includes

Guests and extra guests

How many guests does a rate cover?

Each rate covers a set number of guests: 10 on Matia Sou, 8 on Pearl. Most yachts add a fixed amount for each guest after that, from 1,500 THB on Rose to 10,000 THB on Simon, up to the maximum.

How do I book, and how do I pay?

Send us your date, your group size and the kind of day on WhatsApp or Telegram. Sending a message books nothing. We reply with the yachts that fit, each with its rate. Payment is by bank transfer or cash.

National park fees

Do we pay national park fees on top?

Yes. Each guest pays in cash at the park, and your captain or hostess helps with the local formalities. These are the published fees for foreign visitors, based on Thailand's Department of National Parks regulations.

What to bring

What should we bring?

Swimwear, sunscreen, sunglasses, a change of clothes and your passport for the national parks. Towels are on board. Thailand's marine national parks ban some sunscreens, so choose a reef-safe one.

Places and weather

If the weather turns

What if the sea is rough on the day?

The sea is calmest from November to April. If the sea is too rough, there is no trip that day. In light rain the captain picks a calmer route, or the day moves to another date, subject to availability.

Islands in one day

Which islands can we reach in one day?

Phi Phi, the 4 Islands, Racha, Hong Island, Khai, Maiton and Phang Nga Bay all fit a day of 8 hours. Similan and Surin are overnight charters of 2 to 4 days, planned with us case by case.

More than one day

Can we charter for more than one day?

Yes. Song of Songs, a 42 m yacht for up to 12 guests, is USD 46,000 for two days and one night, all inclusive. Similan and Surin are overnight charters of 2 to 4 days, quoted case by case.

When to go

When should we go?

November to April is the calmest and clearest. Maya Bay closes 1 August to 30 September. The Similan and Surin Islands are open 15 October to 15 May, and closed in the monsoon.
