What to expect Yachts When to go Questions

Off Railay the water turns clear and shallow over coral, and the islands stand close together: Koh Poda's white beach and its limestone stack, the bird-head rock of Chicken Island, and at low tide a strip of white sand that joins Tup Island to its neighbours.

Poda Island

A white beach, clear water and a limestone stack offshore, with shade, food stalls and restrooms on the sand.

Chicken Island

Named for the rock that looks like a chicken's head, clearest from the water on its south-east side.

Tup Island

At low tide a sandbar links Koh Kai, Koh Tup and Koh Mor, and you walk between them over shallow, turquoise water.

Railay Beach

At the tip of the Railay headland, with no road to it, Phra Nang Cave Beach: a palm-fringed bay with the Princess Cave and its shrine at the south end.

November to early May is the season for Thale Waek. The south-west monsoon covers Thailand from about mid-May to mid-October.

Questions

Can you walk between the islands?

At low tide, yes: the Thale Waek sandbar joins Koh Kai, Koh Tup and Koh Mor, and you walk across in shallow water. At high tide it is under the sea.
